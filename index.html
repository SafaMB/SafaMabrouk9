About Me

I'm a UI/UX Designer and Computer Science Engineer with experience across UX research, product design, UI design, and software development.

My approach combines an understanding of users, business needs, and technical constraints to transform complex requirements into clear and intuitive digital experiences.

I work across the design process, from understanding user needs and defining flows to wireframing, prototyping, UI design, and usability testing.

My technical background in software development also helps me collaborate effectively with developers and design solutions that are both usable and technically feasible.

I'm particularly interested in digital products, business platforms, and technology-driven solutions where design can simplify complex workflows and improve everyday experiences.
